How It Works

4-Phase Market Resolution

1️⃣ Create

Pay 10 PROOF + 100 USDC collateral. Set parameters. Market goes live.

2️⃣ Bet

Users bet YES/NO with USDC. Market-determined odds. Closes at deadline.

3️⃣ Proof

Creator submits verifiable proof (HTTPS/IPFS). Community reviews.

4️⃣ Vote

Non-bettors vote YES/NO/INVALID. Winners get payouts automatically.
